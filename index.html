○ ConceptCharger diagnostics

Pilot-line analysis (CP / PP)

Industry reliability studies still see roughly one charging attempt in seven fail — most before charging even starts, where uptime metrics are blind. Vehicle–charger communication and connector locking dominate those technical failures. Pilot-line analysis targets exactly this zone: vehicle not detected, lock fault, pilot fault, mid-session abort — separated into charger-side and vehicle-side causes in IEC 61851 / ISO 15118 sessions.

How we work

Measure it, then explain it.

01

Hardware at the source

Small, isolated, field-mountable instruments that sit on the signal itself — not a proxy of it — and survive the cabinet.

02

Software that finds the pattern

Events are only useful in context. We trend, correlate and classify across devices and sites, so the answer is a cause, not a log line.

03

Built for European infrastructure

Designed around IEC 61851 / ISO 15118 / OCPP, CE and CRA obligations, and the way CPOs, service companies and DSOs actually operate.

About

Founded by an engineer who has stood in front of too many "faulted" cabinets.

Morgen Tech started with a familiar frustration: fast chargers that fail intermittently, get power-cycled, and fail again — with nothing in any log to explain why. Years of chasing such faults showed that the answer usually sits in the electrical layer no monitoring system watches. RailSentinel-24 is the instrument we always wished we had in the van.

Behind it is fifteen years in EV charging and power electronics: charger platforms, communication standards, certification and compliance. We know what the inside of the cabinet looks like, and what the operator on the other end of the phone actually needs.
